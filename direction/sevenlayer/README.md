# Sevenlayer Direction Notes

`sevenlayer/` is directional guidance, not an internal ACTA specification.

## How to use

- Extract reusable principles from Sevenlayer material.
- Adapt concepts to ACTA constraints and authority boundaries.
- Avoid copying Sevenlayer technology stack choices directly into ACTA by default.

## Authority boundary

Sevenlayer references do not directly prescribe ACTA's internal constitutional or architectural categories. Any binding ACTA rule must be rewritten under `constitution/`, `architecture/`, or `decisions/`.
