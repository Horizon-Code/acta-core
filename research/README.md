# Research

Exploratory, comparative, extracted, and non-normative material.

## Belongs here

- Imported notes and analysis drafts.
- Open questions and comparison documents.

## Does not belong here

- Binding architecture rules.
- Accepted decisions.

## Relationship to other folders

Research can inform architecture and decisions but does not define them.

## Documents

- `open-questions.md` — open questions, including those raised by E0.
- `E0-resultados.md` — deliverable of the E0 gate. Empty until the experiment runs; the gate
  stays closed while any of the four decisions is unwritten. Protocol lives in
  `roadmap/E0-protocolo-y-enmiendas.md`.
- `runtime-and-llm-control-notes.md` — runtime/LLM control notes.
- `imported-notes/` — preserved notes from the earlier repository layout.
